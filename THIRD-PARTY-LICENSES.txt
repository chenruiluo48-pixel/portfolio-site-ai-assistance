Hero background: Video.mp4 supplied by the website owner.
The original is archived with the source at assets/hero-original.mp4.
The website plays a muted 1920x1080 H.264 version at media/hero-video.mp4,
with a WebP poster and the original 30fps frame rate and duration.

Portrait: original JPEG supplied by the website owner, copied unchanged.
Project images: AI-generated using built-in imagegen at the owner's request.
They are illustrative technical renders, not photographs of the actual builds
or verified circuit schematics. Original PNGs and prompts are archived with
the development source in assets/generated-projects/, IMAGE-PROMPTS.md and
IMAGE-PROMPTS-BRIGHT.md. The current bright illustrations were generated using
the owner's supplied white-and-blue workflow image as a style reference.

Capability illustrations: three new images generated using built-in imagegen at
the owner's request, in the established bright white-and-blue style. They are
conceptual images for power electronics, embedded control, and modelling/testing,
not photographs of actual work, verified schematics, or measurement evidence.
Originals: assets/generated-capabilities/. Final prompts: IMAGE-PROMPTS-CAPABILITIES.md.
